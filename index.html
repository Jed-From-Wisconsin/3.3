Supreme Court of Gaming

Welcome to the Supreme Court of Gaming. Every game has a case to make, and it's our intention to hear it.

From masterpieces worthy of fame to crimes against the medium, we put games on trial and deliver the verdict they deserve.

Gameplay is Evidence. Reviews are trials. Verdicts are final.

So take your seat, silence your phones, and prepare for judgement. Court is now in session.

How It Works:

The Investigation:

We dive into every game, examine its gameplay, story, visuals, performance, and overall experience. No detail goes unnoticed, and no reputation gets a free pass.

The Trial

We weigh the strengths against the weaknesses- what things are done right, and what is done wrong. We separate genuine innovation from empty promises to determine whether a game lives up to its potential.

The Verdeict

After reviewing all the evidence, we deliver our final judgment. Whether a game is a masterpiece or a complete disaster, every score has a reason behind it.

Justice is Served

Our verdict helps you decide which games deserve your time and money. The final decision is yours, but we'll make sure you have the evidence you need.

The Court's Final Word

No game is above the law. No hype is above scrutiny. Every verdict is earned.
