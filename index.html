Critic's Assembly

Welcome to the Critic's Assembly, the world's first website for democratic game critique. Your time and money are valuable, which is why we're devoted to bringing representation to the games journalist space. Our volunteers bring a variety of perspectives to the games they review, bringing deep, in-depth reflections other outlets can't.

How It Works:

The Critic's Assembly relies on an extensive network of contributors who share their insights, critique gameplay, and explore the artistry behind the games on the market.
